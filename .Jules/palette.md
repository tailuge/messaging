## 2025-03-24 - Settings Micro-UX Improvements
**Learning:** Adding descriptive labels to abstract numeric settings (like L.O.D.) significantly improves clarity. Sectioning a long list of settings with icons and titles makes the interface more scannable. Providing immediate visual feedback for "copy to clipboard" actions (like a "Copied!" badge) reduces user uncertainty.
**Action:** Always prefer descriptive labels over raw numbers for sliders. Use sections/headers for modals with more than 3-4 items.
